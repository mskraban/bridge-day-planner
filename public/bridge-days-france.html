How it works

  1. Choose your country, the year and how many leave days you have.
  2. The planner finds the days to take off so that public holidays and weekends join into long breaks.
  3. Print the calendar, add the breaks to your own calendar, or share the link.

Kako deluje

  1. Izberite državo, leto in število dni dopusta.
  2. Načrtovalnik poišče dneve za dopust, da se prazniki in vikendi združijo v dolge počitke.
  3. Natisnite koledar, dodajte počitke v svoj koledar ali delite povezavo.

Questions

What is a bridge day?

A working day that sits between a public holiday and a weekend. Taking just that day off gives you a long break for the price of one leave day.

How does the planner pick the days?

It checks every way of spending your leave days and keeps the plan that gives you the most days off inside breaks of the length you choose. It never uses more leave than you have, and it never takes leave on a holiday or a weekend.

Where do the holidays come from?

From the free Nager.Date holiday service. Always compare with your employer's calendar, because company days and some regional holidays can differ.

Does it work if my weekend is not Saturday and Sunday?

Yes. Tap the days you work. For example, choose Sunday to Thursday if your weekend is Friday and Saturday.

Is anything stored about me?

Only on your own device: your language and the holiday list, so the page loads faster next time. There are no accounts and no cookies. The hosted site counts anonymous page views with Vercel Web Analytics.

Kaj je mostiček?

Delovni dan med praznikom in vikendom. Če vzamete le ta dan prosto, dobite dolg počitek za ceno enega dneva dopusta.

Kako načrtovalnik izbere dneve?

Preveri vse načine porabe dni dopusta in obdrži načrt, ki da največ prostih dni v počitkih izbrane dolžine. Nikoli ne porabi več dopusta, kot ga imate, in nikoli ne vzame dopusta na praznik ali vikend.

Od kod so prazniki?

Iz brezplačne storitve Nager.Date. Vedno primerjajte s koledarjem svojega delodajalca, ker se lahko podjetniški dnevi in nekateri regionalni prazniki razlikujejo.

Ali deluje, če vikend ni sobota in nedelja?

Da. Izberite dneve, ko delate. Če je vaš vikend petek in sobota, izberite od nedelje do četrtka.

Se o meni kaj shranjuje?

Samo na vaši napravi: jezik in seznam praznikov, da se stran naslednjič naloži hitreje. Ni računov in ni piškotkov. Gostovana stran šteje anonimne oglede z Vercel Web Analytics.
